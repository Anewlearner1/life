# S1 雨夜摔倒｜Google Flow：Frames to Video（首幀＋尾幀）　v1

- 模型：Veo 3.1 Lite｜8 秒｜16:9｜720p
- 首幀：`04_frames/shot_01_start.png`　尾幀：`04_frames/shot_01_end.png`
- 後期取用：生成第 0.3–5.63 秒（摔倒約在生成第 1.2 秒）；固定機位。
- 預算：10 點（已用 40；送出後剩 20，再留給 S6 10 點）。
- 措辭依 S2 經驗：不寫年齡、不寫「濕透的衣服／身體」等身體或衣物細節，只寫「暴雨中的跑者」。

## English prompt（直接貼進 Flow）

```text
[Style] Japanese animated-film look: cel-shaded character with clean linework, a softly painted detailed background, dramatic warm orange backlight on a rainy night. Style, colors and character design stay exactly identical to the first frame and the last frame throughout.

[Camera] 8 seconds total. A low camera close to the ground, slightly from the front-left, a wide shot that keeps the runner's whole body in the frame. Locked-off static camera: no push, no pull, no pan, no cut.

[Scene] A rainstorm on an athletics track at night. The track is dark and glossy, with puddles reflecting warm light. At the back right a tall floodlight shines orange straight toward the camera, creating a strong backlight and a glowing halo of misty rain behind the runner. Rain falls almost vertically, tilting very slightly toward the lower right, with bright droplets and small splashes. Blurred dark stands and trees on the left. Match the first frame exactly.

[Character] "Xiao Qing": a runner with a single high black-brown ponytail, a dark navy sleeveless running top, black running shorts, and worn white running shoes with a blue band (the shoes stay in shadow, and no hole is ever visible on them). She is exhausted and in pain, her face is turned toward the camera or down.

[Timeline]
0–0.2 s: the picture is completely still, identical to the first frame: she is running toward the camera in mid-stride, exhausted, with the floodlight glowing behind her.
0.2–1.2 s: she keeps running toward the camera, getting slightly closer, her stride faltering.
1.2–2.2 s: her legs give way; she stumbles forward and drops onto her knees, both hands landing flat on the track in front of her, with water splashing around her hands and knees.
2.2–5.0 s: she stays kneeling with both hands on the track, her head bowed, breathing hard, her shoulders rising and falling slowly while the rain keeps falling.
5.0–7.7 s: she stays in the same kneeling pose, only breathing.
Last 0.3 s: everything stops completely, and the picture is identical to the last frame: kneeling on the track with both hands on the ground and her head bowed.

[Sound] Only heavy rain, water splashing under her steps, and her labored breathing. No speech. No background music.

[Constraints] Only one person appears. Her whole body stays inside the frame the whole time: she never leaves the frame and never runs into the camera. Her face and body never deform, and she wears two shoes at all times. The floodlight, stands, trees and track stay unchanged; the rain keeps falling the whole time. No new people or objects. Clean image: no text, no captions, no watermark, no logos.
```

## 送出後

3. 有問題告訴我時間點；先看能不能剪掉或用程式修，再問你是否重拍。
