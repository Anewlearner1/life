# S4 夕陽草地睜眼｜Google Flow：Frames to Video（首幀＋尾幀）　v1

- 模型：Veo 3.1 Lite｜長度：8 秒｜畫幅：16:9｜720p
- 首幀：`04_frames/shot_04_start.png`
- 尾幀：`04_frames/shot_04_end.png`
- 後期取用：生成第 0.3–5.63 秒（睜眼約在生成第 2.97 秒，對片中 0:18.67）。**固定機位**；5% 極慢拉遠只在後期對成片做（髮頂未被裁）。
- 預算：10 點（全片 70 點的第 4 次；送出後剩 30 點）。

## 中文底稿
【畫風】日系動畫電影質感：角色賽璐珞上色、線條俐落，背景是細緻手繪的草地，夏日傍晚暖橘色夕陽光。整段畫風、顏色、人物設計與首幀和尾幀完全一致。
【鏡頭】整段 8 秒，正上方俯拍，頭肩加上胸的特寫，固定機位，不推不拉不搖，不切鏡。
【構圖與配色】深橄欖綠的草地；暖橘紅夕陽從畫面右側斜照，右側的草較亮，左側有長長的影子；太陽不入鏡。小晴仰躺，頭朝畫面上方、臉朝上，眼睛在畫面右側約 62%。完全對齊首幀。
【人物】小晴：十七歲女生，仰躺，黑褐色單一高馬尾散在頭後的草地上（髮圈小而深色），齊瀏海，深藍無袖背心；手不入鏡；額頭有一顆小汗珠。
【逐秒】
0–0.2 秒：畫面完全靜止，與首幀一模一樣：閉著眼、嘴微張大口喘氣。
0.2–2.5 秒：她閉著眼，胸口與肩膀緩慢、小幅度地起伏，大口喘氣。
2.5–4.5 秒：她慢慢睜開眼睛，看向正上方的天空；嘴巴慢慢閉上，嘴角輕輕上揚，露出淺淺的微笑。
4.5–7.7 秒：微笑保持，呼吸漸漸平緩，胸口與肩膀起伏更小；眼睛一直睜著，看著天空。
最後 0.3 秒：完全停住，畫面與尾幀一致：睜眼、淺笑、嘴閉。
【聲音】只有輕微的風吹草聲與漸漸平緩的呼吸聲。人物不說話。無背景音樂。
【約束】臉朝上不轉動、不抬頭，五官不顛倒、不變形，只睜眼一次、不眨眼多次；頭、馬尾與頭髮位置幾乎不動，只有極輕微飄動；草葉只往畫面左側輕輕擺動；夕陽從畫面右側，太陽不入鏡；額頭的汗珠不增不減；手不入鏡。畫面裡只有小晴一個人。畫面乾淨，無文字、無字幕、無浮水印。

## English prompt（直接貼進 Flow）

```text
[Style] Japanese animated-film look: cel-shaded character with clean linework, a softly painted detailed grass background, warm orange summer-sunset light. Style, colors and character design stay exactly identical to the first frame and the last frame throughout.

[Camera] 8 seconds total. Straight top-down view from directly above, a close-up of head, shoulders and upper chest. Locked-off static camera: no push, no pull, no pan, no cut.

[Composition and color] Dark olive-green grass. Warm orange-red sunset light comes from the right side of the frame at a low angle: the grass on the right is brighter and long shadows fall to the left; the sun itself is not visible. Xiao Qing lies on her back with her head toward the top of the frame and her face pointing up, her eyes at about 62% of the frame width from the left. Match the first frame exactly.

[Character] "Xiao Qing": a 17-year-old girl lying on her back, her single high black-brown ponytail loosely spread on the grass behind her head (a small dark hair tie), straight bangs, a dark navy sleeveless tank top; her hands are not visible; one small bead of sweat on her forehead.

[Timeline]
0–0.2 s: the picture is completely still, identical to the first frame: eyes closed, mouth slightly open, panting heavily.
0.2–2.5 s: with her eyes closed, her chest and shoulders rise and fall slowly with small movements as she pants.
2.5–4.5 s: she slowly opens her eyes and looks straight up at the sky; her mouth slowly closes and the corners of her mouth lift into a soft, gentle smile.
4.5–7.7 s: the smile stays; her breathing gradually calms and the rise and fall of her chest and shoulders gets even smaller; her eyes stay open, looking at the sky.
Last 0.3 s: everything stops completely, and the picture is identical to the last frame: eyes open, a faint smile, mouth closed.

[Sound] Only soft wind in the grass and her breathing slowly calming down. No speech. No background music.

[Constraints] Her face stays pointing up: she does not turn her head or lift it, her features never flip or deform, she opens her eyes only once and does not blink repeatedly. Her head, ponytail and hair stay almost exactly in place with only very slight movement. The grass blades only sway gently toward the left of the frame. The sunset light comes from the right and the sun never appears in the frame. The bead of sweat on her forehead neither increases nor disappears. No hands in the frame. Only Xiao Qing appears. Clean image: no text, no captions, no watermark.
```

## 送出後
1. 存成 `06_clips/shot_04_v1.mp4`（新版本一律存新檔名）。
2. **請你親自看動態**：睜眼是否自然（不是瞬間跳開或多次眨眼）、臉有沒有變形或轉動、肩膀有沒有整個上半身晃動、聲音是否乾淨。
3. 有問題告訴我時間點；先看能不能剪掉或用程式修，再問你是否重拍。
