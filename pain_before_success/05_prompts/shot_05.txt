## 中文底稿

【畫風】日系動畫電影質感：角色賽璐珞上色、線條俐落，背景是細緻手繪、帶柔焦與金色逆光光暈，暖色金色夕陽光。整段畫風、顏色、人物設計與首幀和尾幀完全一致。
【鏡頭】整段 8 秒，整段戲劇性慢動作（約 0.4 倍速），全景，低機位、中長焦，固定機位，不推不拉不搖，不切鏡。
【構圖與配色】暖橘褐色跑道、白色分道線；右上方遠處是金色逆光的太陽與暖橘天空，左側是看台，右側是樹林；一條白色終點帶橫跨跑道中段。影子往畫面左前方，風往畫面左後方。完全對齊首幀。
【人物】小晴：十七歲女生，全黑單一高馬尾（深色小髮圈）、齊瀏海、深藍無袖背心、胸前一塊純白素面號碼布（無字、無數字、無別針）、黑色短褲（無開衩）、純紅跑鞋配白底（無標誌、無記號）。兩名對手：比小晴更小、離鏡頭更遠、稍微失焦，一人灰上衣深灰短褲、一人白上衣黃短褲，沒有任何紅色，步態不同。
【逐秒】
0–0.2 秒：畫面完全靜止，與首幀一模一樣。
0.2–3.8 秒：小晴咬牙全力衝刺，沿著斜線從畫面左後方朝右前方跑向終點帶，從首幀的大小平順、逐漸地變大；頭髮與馬尾被風吹向畫面左後方。兩名對手一直離鏡頭比她更遠、在畫面左側，速度比她慢，全程在從鏡頭看去的終點帶後方、尚未過線，只會因為往前跑而稍微變大，不靠近她、不交叉。
3.8–4.3 秒：她的胸口撞上終點帶，帶子在被撞到的位置斷成兩截；接著雙臂順勢向兩側張開。
4.3–7.7 秒：她繼續往右前方跑，雙臂張開、頭微微後仰，表情慢慢轉成喜悅；斷成兩截的帶子先隨她往前飄，再往畫面左右兩側垂落，一直留在畫面中，不消失。對手仍沒有過線。
最後 0.3 秒：所有人完全停住，畫面與尾幀完全一致。
【聲音】只有輕微的風聲與腳步聲。人物不說話。無背景音樂。無觀眾歡呼。
【約束】畫面裡自始至終只有首幀這三個人（小晴與兩名對手）與終點帶；臉與身形全程不變。小晴只會從首幀大小平順地變大到尾幀大小，過程中不突然放大、絕不比尾幀更大，不衝向鏡頭。背景（看台、樹、跑道分道線、太陽）保持不變，不新增人物與物品。除了紅鞋以外，任何地方都沒有紅色或粉色配件。畫面乾淨，無文字、無字幕、無浮水印、無商標。

## English prompt（直接貼進 Flow）

```text
[Style] Japanese animated-film look: cel-shaded characters with clean linework, a softly painted detailed background with gentle depth-of-field and a golden backlight glow, warm golden-hour light. Style, colors and character design stay exactly identical to the first frame and the last frame throughout.

[Camera] 8 seconds total, the whole shot in dramatic slow motion (about 0.4x speed). Wide shot, low angle, medium-telephoto look. Locked-off static camera: no push, no pull, no pan, no cut.

[Composition and color] Warm orange-tan running track with white lane lines. Far upper right: a golden backlit sun and a warm orange sky. Stands on the left, trees on the right. One white finish-line tape stretches across the middle of the track. Shadows fall toward the left-front of the frame; wind blows toward the left-rear. Match the first frame exactly.

[Characters] "Xiao Qing": a 17-year-old girl, a single high jet-black ponytail with a small plain dark hair tie, straight bangs, dark navy sleeveless running tank top, a plain pure-white race bib on her chest (no text, no numbers, no pins), plain black shorts (no side slits), completely plain bright red running shoes with white soles (no logos, no marks). Two opponents: smaller than Xiao Qing, farther from the camera, slightly out of focus; one in a gray top and dark gray shorts, one in a white top and yellow shorts; no red on them; they run with different strides.

[Timeline]
0–0.2 s: the picture is completely still, identical to the first frame.
0.2–3.8 s: Xiao Qing sprints at full effort with a gritted-teeth expression, running diagonally from the left-rear toward the right-front, toward the finish tape, smoothly and gradually growing from her size in the first frame; her hair and ponytail are blown toward the left-rear. The two opponents always stay farther from the camera than Xiao Qing, on the left side of the frame, slower than her, behind the finish tape from the camera's view and have not crossed it; they grow only slightly larger as they run forward, never approach her and never cross her path.
3.8–4.3 s: her chest hits the finish tape and the tape snaps at the point where her chest hits it, into two pieces; then her arms swing open to both sides.
4.3–7.7 s: she keeps running toward the right-front with both arms spread wide and her head tilted slightly back, her expression slowly turning to joy; the two pieces of the broken tape first drift forward with her, then droop down toward the left and right sides of the frame, and stay visible in the frame, never disappearing. The opponents still have not crossed the line.
Last 0.3 s: everything stops completely, and the picture is identical to the last frame.

[Sound] Only soft wind and light footsteps. No speech. No background music. No crowd cheering.

[Constraints] From start to finish only the three runners from the first frame (Xiao Qing and two opponents) and the finish tape appear. Faces and body shapes never change. Xiao Qing only grows smoothly from her size in the first frame to her size in the last frame: she never suddenly enlarges, never becomes larger than in the last frame, and never runs into the camera. The background (stands, trees, lane lines, sun) stays unchanged; no new people or objects. No red or pink accessories anywhere except the shoes. Clean image: no text, no captions, no watermark, no logos.
```
